Level 2: Sorting & Limiting (11–20)
Sort by cgpa ascending.
db.students.find().sort({cgpa:1})
---------------------------------
Sort by cgpa descending.
db.students.find().sort({cgpa:-1})
------------------------------------
Top 5 students.
db.students.find().sort({cgpa:-1}).limit(5)
--------------------------------------------
Lowest cgpa.
db.students.find().sort({cgpa:1}).limit(1)
--------------------------------------------
Oldest student.
db.students.find().sort({age:-1}).limit(1)
-----------------------------------------
Youngest student.
db.students.find().sort({age:1}).limit(1)
---------------------------------------
Skip first 10 students.
db.students.find().skip(10)
-----------------------------------------
Latest created student.
db.students.find().sort({createdAt:-1}).limit(1)
---------------------------
Earliest created student.
db.students.find().sort({createdAt:1}).limit(1)
--------------------------------
Top 3 CSE students.
db.students.find({department:'CSE'}).sort({cgpa:-1}).limit(3)
