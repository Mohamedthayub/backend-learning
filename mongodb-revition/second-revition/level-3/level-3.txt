Level 3 – Sorting & Limiting
Exercise 17

Sort employees by salary (ascending).
db.employees.find().sort({salary:1})

Exercise 18

Sort employees by salary (descending).
db.employees.find().sort({salary:-1})

Exercise 19

Display the top 5 highest-paid employees.
db.employees.find().sort({salary:-1}).limit(5)

Exercise 20

Skip the first 3 employees and display the rest.
db.employees.find().skip(3)
