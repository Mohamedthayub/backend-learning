Find employees whose salary is greater than 50000.
db.employees.find({salary:{$gt:50000}})

Exercise 31

Find employees whose salary is less than 40000.
 db.employees.find({salary:{$lt:40000}})
Exercise 32

Find employees whose age is greater than or equal to 30.
db.employees.find({age:{$gte:30}})
Exercise 33

Find employees whose age is not equal to 25.

db.employees.find({age:{$ne:25}})
Exercise 34

Find employees whose department is either IT or HR.
db.employees.find({$or:[{departmen:'IT'},{department:'HR'}]})

Exercise 35

Find employees whose city is not Chennai
db.employees.find({city:{$ne:'Chennai'}})
