Level 4 – Update Operations
Exercise 21

Update one employee's city.
db.employees.updateOne({name:'Aisha'},{$set:{city:'TEN'}})

Exercise 22

Increase one employee's salary.
db.employees.updateOne({name:'Aisha'},{$inc:{salary:2000}})

Exercise 23

Increase every employee's salary by 5000.
db.employees.updateMany({},{$inc:{salary:5000}})

Exercise 24

Rename the department "Sales" to "Marketing".
db.employees.updateOne({name:'Vikram'},{$set:{'Sales':'Marketing'}})
Exercise 25

Remove the city field from one employee.
db.employees.updateOne({name:'Aisha'},{$unset:{city:''}})

Exercise 26

Add an email field to every employee
db.employees.updateMany({},{$set:{email:''}})
