Level 8 – Array Fields

Add a new field:

skills


Example:

["JavaScript","Node.js","MongoDB"]
 db.employees.updateMany({},{$set:{skills:["JavaScript","Node.js","MongoDB"]
| }})
Exercise 40

Find employees who know MongoDB.
db.employees.find({skills:'MongoDB'})


Exercise 41

Find employees having JavaScript and Express.
 db.employees.find({skills:{$in:['Javascript','Express']}})
Exercise 42

Add React to an employee's skills.
db.employees.updateMany({},{$push:{skills:'React'}})

Exercise 43

Remove HTML from an employee's skills.
db.employees.updateMany({},{$pull:{skills:'HTML'}})

Exercise 44

Count employees having Node.js.
db.employees.find({skills:{$in:['Node.js']}}).count()
