Level 7 – Logical Operators
Exercise 36

Find employees working in IT and earning more than 60000.
db.employees.find({$and:[{department:'IT'},{salary:{$gt:60000}}]})
Exercise 37

Find employees from Chennai or Bangalore.
db.employees.find({$or:[{city:'Chennai'},{city:'Bangalore'}]})
Exercise 38

Find employees not in Sales.

db.employees.find({department:{$not:{$eq:'Sales'}}})
Exercise 39

Find employees older than 25 and younger than 35.
 db.employees.find({$and:[{age:{$gt:25,$lt:35}}]})
