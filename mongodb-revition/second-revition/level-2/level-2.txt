Level 2 – Read Operations
Exercise 4

Display all employees.
db.employees.find()
Exercise 5

Display only employee names.
db.employees.find({},{_id:0,name:1})

Exercise 6

Display only name and department.
db.employees.find({},{_id:0,name:1,department:1})

Exercise 7

Find employees working in the IT department.
db.employees.find({department:'IT'})
Exercise 8

Find employees living in Chennai.
db.employees.find({city:'Chennai'})

Exercise 9

Find employees older than 30.
 db.employees.find({age: {$gt:30}})
Exercise 10

Find employees whose salary is greater than 60000.
db.employees.find({salary: {$gt:60000}})

Exercise 11

Find employees aged between 25 and 35.
db.employees.find({age:{$gt:25 , $lt:35}})

Exercise 12

Find employees from Chennai or Bangalore.
db.employees.find({$or:[{city:'Chennai'},{city:'Bangalore'}]})

Exercise 13

Find employees not working in HR.
db.employees.find({department:{$not:{$eq:'HR'}}})

Exercise 14

Find employees whose name starts with "A".
db.employees.find({name:{$regex:'^A'}})

Exercise 15

Find employees whose name ends with "n".
db.employees.find({name:{$ :'n$'}})
Exercise 16

Find employees whose name contains "ar".
db.employees.find({name:{$regex:'ar'}})
