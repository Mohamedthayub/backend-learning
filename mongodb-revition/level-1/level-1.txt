Level 1: Basic Queries (1–10)
Find all students.
db.students.find()
-----------------
Find only name and cgpa.
db.students.find({},{name:1},{cgpa:1})
------------------------------------
Find students older than 20.
db.students.find({age:{$gt:20}})
----------------------------------
Find students from Chennai.
db.students.find({city:'Chennai'})
--------------------------------------
Find students with cgpa greater than 8.
db.students.find({cgpa:{$gt:8}})
------------------------------------
Find inactive students.
db.students.find({active:false})
-------------------------------------
Find students in CSE department.
db.students.find({department:'CSE'})
--------------------------------------
Find students whose name starts with A.
db.students.find({name:/^A/})
------------------------------------
Find students whose city contains "pur".
db.students.find({city:/pur/})
-----------------------------------------
Find students aged between 18 and 22.
db.students.find({age:{$gt:18, $lt:22}})
------------------------------------------
