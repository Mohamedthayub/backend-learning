Level 3: Update Queries (21–30)
Update one student's city.
db.students.updateOne({name:'Nisha'},{$set:{city:'Tirunelveli'}})
----------------------------------------------------------------
Increase cgpa by 0.5.
db.students.updateMany({},{$inc:{cgpa:0.5}})
----------------------------------------------
Add skill "Node.js".
db.students.updateOne({name:'Thayub'},{$push:{skills:'Node.js'}})
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Remove skill "Java".
db.students.updateOne({name:'Thayub'},{$pull:{skills:'Java'}})
--------------------------------------------------------
Rename department field
db.students.updateMany({},{$rename:{department:'branch'}})
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Update multiple students to active.
db.students.updateMany({active:false},{$set:{active:true}})
--------------------------------------------------------------
Change city for all Chennai students.
db.students.updateMany({city:'Chennai'},{$set:{city:'Tirunelveli'}})
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Set graduation year.
db.students.updateMany({},{$set:{'grauduation-year':2025}})
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Remove graduation year.
db.students.updateMany({},{$unset:{'grauduation-year':null}})
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Replace one document completely
db.students.replaceOne({name:'Nisha'},{name:'Thayub',age:21,department:'IT',cgpa:10,city:'Tirunelveli',skills:['DBMS','Dsa','Programming'],active:true,createdAt: ISODate('2024-08-01T00:00:00.000Z')})
