Find the top 5 students by GPA.
db.students.find().sort({gpa:-1}).limit(5)
-------------------------------------------
Find all CSE students.
db.students.find({department:'CSE'})
----------------------------------------------
Find students older than 20.
db.students.find({age:{$gt:20}})
--------------------------------------
Find teachers with more than 10 years of experience.
db.teachers.find({experience:{$gt:10}})
---------------------------------------------
Find courses worth more than 4 credits.
db.courses.find({credits:{$gt:4}})
------------------------------------

Find students who know Node.js.
db.students.find({skills:'Node.js'})
-------------------------------------

---------------------------------
Delete inactive students.
db.students.deleteMany({fullTime:false})
----------------------------------------
Add attendance to every student.
db.students.updateMany({},{$set:{attendance:100}})
--------------------------------------------------
Remove attendance.
db.students.updateMany({},{$unset:{attendance:null}})
--------------------------------------------------
Count students in each department (using aggregation later).
db.students.aggregate([
|     {
|         $group: {
|             _id: "$department",
|             totalStudents: {
|                 $sum: 1
|             }
|         }
|     }
| ])
-----------------------------------------------
Sort students alphabetically.
db.students.find().sort({name:1})
------------------------------
Find the newest students.
db.students.find().sort({age:1}).limit(1)
-------------------------------------
Paginate students (page 2).
db.students.find().skip(5).limit(5)
--------------------------------------
Find students from Chennai with GPA above 8
db.students.find({$and:[{gpa:{$gt:8}},{'address.district':'Chennai'}]})
