Exercises:

Find Chennai students.
db.students.find({'address.city':'Chennai'})
-------------------------------------------
Find students from Tamil Nadu.
db.students.find({'address.state':'Tamil Nadu'})
---------------------------------------------
Update city.
db.students.updateOne({_id:ObjectId('6a4653e9f0bde14a661ed741')},{$set:{'address.city':'tirunelvelu'}})
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Update pincode.
db.students.updateOne({_id:ObjectId('6a4653e9f0bde14a661ed741')},{$set:{'address.pincode':627004}})
-------------------------------------------
Find by city and GPA.
db.students.find({'address.city':'Tirunelveli',gpa:9})
--------------------------------------------------------
Change the whole address.
db.students.updateOne({_id: ObjectId('6a4c3a6adf6a9546702d323d')},{$set:{address:{doorNo:'42a/5 south thaikka street',city:'Tirunelveli',state:'TamilNadu',}}})
------------------------------------------------------
Remove pincode.
db.students.updateOne({name:'Gokul'},{$unset:{pincode:null}})
--------------------------------------------------
Add country.
db.students.updateMany({},{$set:{country:'India'}})
----------------------------------------
Rename city to district.
db.students.updateMany({},{$rename:{'address.city':'District'}})
