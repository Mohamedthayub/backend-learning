Query Operator 
Exercises:

GPA > 8
db.students.find({'gpa':{$gt: 8}})
--------------------------------------
GPA < 6
db.students.find({'gpa':{$lt: 6}})
---------------------------------------
GPA between 7 and 9
db.students.find({'gpa':{$gte: 7,$lte:9}})
---------------------------------------
Age not equal to 20
db.students.find({'age': {$ne:20}})
----------------------------------------
Department is CSE or ECE
db.students.find({$or:[{department:'CSE',department:'ECE'}]})
---------------------------------------------------------
Department is neither CSE nor ECE
db.students.find({$nor:[{department:'CSE',department:'ECE'}]})
 -------------------------------------------------
Full-time AND GPA > 8
db.students.find({fullTime:true,gpa:{$gt:8}})
---------------------------------------------------
Age < 20 OR GPA > 9
db.students.find({$or:[{age:{$lt:20}},{gpa:{$gt:9}}]})
----------------------------------------------
Find documents missing the skills field.
db.students.find({skills:{$exists:false}})
-----------------------------------------------
Find students with department in ["CSE","IT"].
db.students.find({department:{$in:['CSE','IT']}})
--------------------------------------------
Find students whose age is not in [18,19].
db.students.find({age:{$nin:[18,19]}})
------------------------------------------
Use $not with GPA.
db.students.find({gpa:{$not:{$gt:9}}}) // i learnt how to not operator
------------------------------------
Combine three conditions
db.students.find({$and:[{fullTime:true},{age:{$gt:20}},{gpa:{$lt:9}}]})
-------------------------------------------
Find students from Chennai with GPA > 8
db.students.find({$and:[{'address.city':'Chennai'},{gpa:{$gt:8}}]})
------------------------------------
Find non-full-time students 16–25. Create ten more combinations on your own.
db.students.find({fullTime:false})
